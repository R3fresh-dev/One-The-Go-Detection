The current customer description is broad, and no interviews, users, revenue, pilots, or committed partners have been reported. **Technical investor:** The repository provides substantial software execution, including mobile applications, backend, firmware, and model-governance/evaluation tooling. It does not establish a qualified saliva assay, physical sensing performance, or a trained and validated human impairment model. GPT writing the code is compatible with a technical founder; the relevant evidence is Jacob's understanding, design decisions, debugging, and verification. An assay-development path and appropriate scientific expertise remain important dependencies.

**Consumer-business investor:** The intended buyer is an adult seeking useful information after cannabis use. The first recurring problem, existing workaround, willingness to complete the check, testing frequency, purchase trigger, and willingness to pay remain hypotheses. The $20/month subscription needs evidence about both demand and variable costs. Building the integrated research MVP and conducting customer discovery should proceed together.

These are preparation perspectives, not statements from actual YC reviewers or investors. The present application has credible founder potential and limited business and scientific evidence.
