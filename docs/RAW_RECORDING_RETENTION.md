# Optional raw recording and retention

Study consent does not enable raw recording. Eye video and ECG waveform choices start with both `record:false` and `upload:false`. The participant reviews a separate exact optional-purpose notice and approved policy; uploading also requires recording consent. Source laboratory PDFs use a separate authorized administrative purpose and have no participant upload toggle.

The operator's immutable retention submission specifies study, purpose ID/text, allowed kinds, access roles, maximum days, exact UTF-8 notice text/version/SHA256, policy expiry and `delete_live_data` withdrawal behavior. Submission, independent review and independent release are separate maintenance principals. Unreleased, expired, revoked, changed or unaudited material refuses admission. Named retention exceptions are not interpreted as legal holds: this release refuses raw admission for a policy with nonempty exceptions. A legal hold needs a separately implemented and reviewed bounded category/reason/expiry authority.

The participant APIs are:

- `GET /v1/studies/{study_id}/raw-recordings?artifact_cursor={uuid}` returns the approved policy content/digest, notice, per-kind availability, effective choices, exact immutable preference receipt, up to 500 artifact lifecycle entries, a next cursor, and the most recent 100 revocation receipts. Availability is separate from measurement qualification. The response contains no raw bytes or signed upload URL.
- `POST /v1/studies/{study_id}/raw-recording-preferences` accepts `consent_record_id`, `policy_id`, `policy_version`, `policy_sha256`, `raw_notice_version`, `raw_notice_sha256`, `acknowledged_at`, and both kinds' `record`/`upload` choices. The policy hash covers its full canonical approved `content`; notice hash covers exact UTF-8 text. The request requires current notice, generation, recent authentication and idempotency. A changed policy or consent requires a new review. Turning choices off also retires tickets and creates a separate revocation receipt.
- `POST /v1/studies/{study_id}/raw-recording-revocations` accepts `confirmation:true` and a nonempty unique `artifact_kinds` subset. It is a privacy escape without fresh-login or current-notice barriers. Authentication, owner, generation and idempotency checks remain. It immediately retires verification authority, persists a revocation receipt and schedules cleanup. Mobile stores an immediate local stop fence before a queued offline revocation reaches the server.

An immutable grant receipt records exactly what was reviewed and when. A later revocation does not change that receipt: top-level choices describe current effective authority, while `preference_receipt.choices` describes the original grant. Receipts are removed with the applicable account-data generation during privacy deletion.

For participant upload admission, `ArtifactRequest` additionally requires `raw_consent_record_id`, `attempt_id` and `capture_started_at`. The attempt must belong to the same owner, generation and session, with matching component, immutable start and ECG modality where applicable. Its server first-start time and the client's declared acquisition start are checked against the prior grant and bounded clock skew. Retention starts at the earlier of those owned start times. It is frozen before a ticket is returned. A terminal attempt update, storage verification retry, newly approved policy or repeated upload of the same bytes cannot extend it. A previously seen same-generation payload is compared using a keyed HMAC and retains the earlier deadline. No more than 100 live raw lifecycle rows can be admitted per account. Native timing/geometry and the extractor still need independent qualification; these database checks establish software ownership and policy timing only.

For administrative report corrections, the independently authorized `lab_report_revision` role and current consent must match an approved `source_lab_report` policy whose purpose is `administrative_report_revision`. Its conservative lifetime is anchored to the original session creation, rather than a later correction/upload/completion time. Generic participant upload routes cannot grant that purpose.

`Artifact.expires` remains the five-minute upload-ticket deadline. Migration `004_raw_retention` adds an independent lifecycle table with the frozen policy identity and `retained_until`. It never rewrites version 1 scientific evidence. Historical raw objects without independent optional-purpose/retention proof remain represented, are refused fresh authority and are scheduled for fail-closed cleanup; migration does not invent an approval or a lifetime for them.

Verification rechecks current authority before storage reads and inside its final database transaction. A withdrawal, policy revocation, generation change or raw-choice revocation immediately denies verification and finalization. Maintenance detects those changes and elapsed retention. It preserves an existing quarantine version through the frozen signed upload deadline plus 60 seconds so deleting it cannot reopen the conditional `If-None-Match:*` write while its URL is usable. During this window status remains `cleanup_pending`. It then inventories and deletes every exact-key S3 VersionId and delete marker, checks that no versions remain, and settles `purged`. Keys that merely share the prefix are never deleted. A failed dependency or worker crash remains retryable; purged references are checked again every 15 minutes for late in-flight arrivals.

Participant raw completion verifies acquisition provenance as well as the file hash and size. ROI bytes must reproduce the exact accepted optical frame ledger, including decoded dimensions, frame count, sequence, timestamps, each luminance digest and independently extracted statistics. ECG samples must reproduce the accepted signed reader chunks, their exact sample indices, values, units and capture attestation. The immutable owned verification receipt pins the exact S3 version, complete released configuration and accepted evidence digest. A manually marked verified artifact or a receipt from another attempt, account or generation cannot authorize later raw export. These software proofs do not independently qualify the physical sensor.

After physical purge, one-way metadata compaction removes raw SHA256, acquisition times, attempt IDs and consent IDs from the lifecycle authority. Only the frozen write deadline and a same-generation keyed payload fingerprint remain with the policy-independent cleanup key; study/kind remain for the current customer's lifecycle history. Once that account generation is retired, another one-way compaction removes its fingerprint, study and kind. The retained non-health cleanup ledger contains only owner/generation/artifact reference, exact bucket/key, frozen deadlines and cleanup settlement. PostgreSQL guards prevent restoring compacted metadata or changing owner, key or lifetime. This minimal cleanup reference is distinct from retaining raw bytes.

`tests/test_raw_retention.py` covers authority loss, exact receipt/digest/clock checks, deadline nonextension, pending write barriers, exact version/marker deletion, neighboring keys, late arrivals, dependency/crash retry, preserving migration and metadata compaction. `apps/mobile/testfixtures/backend-raw-recordings.json` is generated from an actual fictional `raw_status()` response and is independently consumed by mobile parser tests. `infra/local/raw_version_probe.py` executes real conditional-write and version-delete behavior against a uniquely created disposable LocalStack bucket and removes only that bucket. These are software checks; they do not establish physical camera/BLE timing, scientific validity or production AWS retention behavior.

Interrupted optional raw uploads can request a new five-minute ticket at `POST /v1/artifact-uploads/{artifact_id}/ticket`. The service locks the retention row, rechecks the current account, consent, purpose, configuration and recording/upload choice, and permits only a quarantine artifact with more than six minutes left in its original retention window. Every renewal appends an immutable deadline receipt, keeps the original key, digest and acquisition deadline, and is bounded to 100 renewals. Cleanup retains the conditional-write barrier through the latest issued ticket plus 60 seconds. Renewal never extends raw retention, revives revoked authority, or overwrites verified bytes.

A retention approval alone does not enable recording. Customer availability, fresh recording permission and artifact admission also require the installed configuration's exact implemented raw codec: `optical_roi_v1` with `otgd_eye_roi_v1`, or the signed ECG ADC adapter with `otgd_ecg_samples_v1`, admitted millivolt units and a positive bounded sample limit. Feature-only or unknown adapters show `RAW_CODEC_UNIMPLEMENTED`; a retained old consent receipt cannot bypass that refusal.
