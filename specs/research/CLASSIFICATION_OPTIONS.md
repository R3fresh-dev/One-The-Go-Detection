## Evidence and assumptions

Reviewed the repository README, PRD, decision log, existing FDA assessment and faster-market strategy, and implemented synthetic tools. The product is currently a specification and synthetic integration starter. No actual THC assay, mobile application, production hardware or impairment model is demonstrated.

The user confirmed consumer self-assessment: people use the product themselves to judge whether they are impaired. That is a substantive impairment intended use even if the person makes the final decision. It does not by itself establish medical-device jurisdiction, an exemption, or driving fitness; the exact claims, function and consequences still need assessment. The user confirmed the United States as the first market. It is a scope recommendation, not an agency classification determination. Live retrieval of FDA and eCFR sources failed with proxy 403 denials; the existing reports' specific 2026 regulatory developments and clearance claims have not been independently verified in this session.

## Recommendation

The least-burden service below changes the benefit delivered: it does not tell a consumer whether they are impaired. If that assessment is mandatory for the first release, the full inference function must be assessed on its actual intended use and evidence. No verified exemption for that exact function has been established here.

For the least new device regulatory work, begin as a domestic distributor of unchanged, legally marketed finished tests, with an app restricted to ordering, refills, general education and faithful, source-attributed result history. Confirm each exact SKU's consumer authorization, instructions, supplier relationship and supported workflow. Preserve manufacturer packaging and labeling. A distributor's role and a software function's status are separate questions from the classification of the supplied tests.

If a proprietary integrated pocket device is essential at first sale, pursue a narrower exposure-screening instrument with a qualified OEM. Do not assume that a cleared cartridge authorizes a new collector, optical reader, phone interpretation, combined enclosure or custom app control. Determine responsibility for the finished system and document changes and evidence reuse.

Keep individualized impairment prediction as a separate research program. It adds independent endpoint validation and a potentially different regulatory pathway. Calling the same functionality wellness, entertainment, beta or research does not itself change intended use.

| First offering | Provisional regulatory direction | Work saved and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Administrative/history app only | Potential non-device functions, assessed individually | Avoids developing a test or inference engine; privacy and truthful marketing still apply |
| Unchanged finished tests from a US supplier plus limited app | Domestic distribution plus potentially non-device software | May avoid a new manufacturer submission for those unchanged activities; importing, relabeling, repackaging and kit assembly require separate review |
| Own oral-fluid THC screen | Repository identifies Class II cannabinoid testing under 21 CFR 862.3870; OTC submission assessment required | Narrower than impairment, but a chemical screen is not automatically unregulated |
| Separate breath-alcohol measurement | Repository identifies Class I under 21 CFR 862.3050, conditionally 510(k)-exempt | Exemption limits and other obligations remain; does not exempt THC functions |
| THC plus physiological/task impairment score | Exact classification and predicate remain unresolved | Highest evidence uncertainty; no minimum-bureaucracy classification established |

An employment/insurance-only exemption discussed in the existing reports would require a genuinely different intended use and distribution. Its current conditions must be verified; it cannot be borrowed for a consumer self-test. A supplier authorization does not substantiate safe-to-drive, sobriety, drug-clearance timing or a combined impairment claim.

## Concrete scope to evaluate

Proposed service description: “Manage results and refills for supported THC screening tests and breath-alcohol products.” Match wording to actual supplier labeling. Start with manual, attributed result entry. Omit automatic strip reading, new device control, pupil/cardiac interpretation, personalized intoxication scores and clearance countdowns from that release.

Resolve: first country and state; exact buyer/user; decisions made from results; whether one custom device is mandatory; supplier/SKU; and whether the company distributes domestically, imports, relabels or specifies the device. Prepare a one-page intended-use statement and function/activity matrix before deciding whether an FDA information request is necessary. A focused regulatory review of that concrete scope is more useful than choosing a favorable label for the existing full design.
