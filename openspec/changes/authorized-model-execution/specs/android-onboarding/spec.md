## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: AND-ONB-001 Accurate first-run scope
Onboarding SHALL distinguish guest measurement practice from separately authorized research access. Completion, sign-in and notice acknowledgment SHALL never imply enrollment or model authority. The new binary MAY identify conditional research availability after authenticated server admission. Introductory copy SHALL promise neither accuracy, intoxication classification nor driving clearance.

#### Scenario: Independent research admission
- **WHEN** onboarding completes for an account whose separate current study and channel authority is admitted
- **THEN** the dashboard identifies authorized research access independently of the onboarding receipt

#### Scenario: Legacy or unapproved claim
- **WHEN** a legacy result, local preference or valid-looking manifest claims research authority without current complete admission
- **THEN** no score is admitted and the historical measurement-only or unsupported-result boundary remains in force
