## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: AND-CHECK-007 Result navigation and claim boundaries
Review SHALL offer Save on this device before any explicit account upload. Ordinary, guest and unqualified checks SHALL retain measurement-only outcomes and null scores. The new reader MAY present separately admitted 1.1.0 research results with endpoint, population, uncertainty and original acquisition time. Missing channels and delayed analysis SHALL never become normal findings or driving clearance.

#### Scenario: Qualified research check
- **WHEN** an explicitly uploaded authorized check completes the independently admitted research pipeline
- **THEN** result navigation presents the linked research outcome and its actual acquisition time separately from synchronization status

#### Scenario: Legacy or unapproved claim
- **WHEN** a legacy result, local preference or valid-looking manifest claims research authority without current complete admission
- **THEN** no score is admitted and the historical measurement-only or unsupported-result boundary remains in force
